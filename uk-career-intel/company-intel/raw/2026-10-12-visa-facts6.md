  - "Occupations with a minimum annual remuneration of **€40,904** for a restricted number of strategically important occupations contained in the Critical Skills Occupations List a relevant degree qualification or higher is required."
  - "Where the applicant has received their qualification within the 12 month period prior to the date of application …, the minimum annual remuneration will be **€36,848**."
  - "All occupations with a minimum annual remuneration of over **€68,911**, other than those on the Ineligible List … A non-EEA national who does not have a degree qualification or higher, must have the necessary level of experience."

#### 2a. 英國
- **短期訪問：免簽，但需要 ETA。**
  - Appendix Visitor: Visa national list（https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-visitor-visa-national-list，頁面更新 2026-03-05）："Taiwan*" 雖在 visa national 名單上，但 VN 2.x 例外寫明："nationals or citizens of Taiwan who hold a passport issued by Taiwan that includes in it the number of the identification card issued by the competent authority in Taiwan" 不需要 visit visa。
  - Appendix ETA National List（https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-eta-national-list，更新 2026-03-05）："(c) for travel to the UK on or after 8 January 2025: … Taiwan* … *Those who hold a passport issued by Taiwan that includes in it the number of the identification card…"
  - ETA 概覽（https://www.gov.uk/eta）："An ETA costs £20"，可停留 "up to 6 months"。
- **TB 檢測：不需要。** https://www.gov.uk/tb-test-visa 的 "Countries where you need a TB test" 名單有 China、Hong Kong 等，**沒有 Taiwan**（2026-10-12 全文搜尋 "Taiwan" 為 0 筆）。所以以台灣居民身分申請 6 個月以上簽證時，依此頁不需要 TB 證明。
- **Student visa 的台灣相關規定**：https://www.gov.uk/student-visa 的 "differential evidence requirement" 國家名單**包含 Taiwan**："You do not need to prove you have enough money to support yourself if you're … from one of the following countries or territories: … Taiwan …"，但 "you might be asked to provide this evidence"。ATAS 頁面只寫 "if your course and nationality require it"。MSc Software Development 是否需要 ATAS：**未驗證**（以學校 CAS 為準）。
- **Graduate visa 期限**（https://www.gov.uk/graduate-visa，2026-10-12）：
  - "2 years if you apply on or before 31 December 2026"
  - "**18 months if you apply on or after 1 January 2027**"
  - "If you have a PhD … it will last for 3 years."
  - 費用："18 months - it will cost £1,152.50"
  - 推論：Dennis 2028 年才畢業，適用 **18 個月**。
- **Skilled Worker 一般門檻**（https://www.gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa/your-job，2026-10-12）：
  - "The minimum salary … is whichever is the highest of: **£41,700 per year** [or] the 'going rate'"
  - 職務須是 "on the list of eligible occupations"。2025-07-22 以前取得 CoS 的人有過渡規則。
  - £41,700 的生效日：本頁沒有寫，**未驗證**。
- **New entrant 規則**（同一頁）："You can be paid 70% of your job's standard going rate if your salary will be at least **£33,400** per year and one of the following applies: you're under 26 … you're currently in the UK on a Student visa studying at bachelor's degree level or above (or … in the last 2 years) … you're currently in the UK on a Graduate visa (or … in the last 2 years…)"
  - "Your total stay in the UK cannot be more than **4 years** if you apply for one of these reasons. This includes any time you've already spent in the UK on a Graduate visa."
